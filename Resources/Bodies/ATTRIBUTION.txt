Based on NASA imagery and elevation data. Modified by Organic: resized,
projected, lit, and rendered as static direction-lit sprites.

Ceres, Haumea, and Eris use artist-created maps from Solar System Scope;
they are not observed surface maps.

Pluto color map (PIA11707): NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics
Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute.
Public-domain NASA imagery (United States), used under the JPL Image Use Policy.
https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia11707-pluto-color-map/
https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/
Modified by Organic: resized; unobserved map gaps filled for display;
projected and lit. The observed heart region is retained.

Pallas, Juno, Vesta, Astraea, Hygiea, Chiron, Pholus, Nessus, Orcus, Quaoar,
Makemake, Gonggong, and Sedna use procedural surface illustrations created
for Organic from the approved PlanetBodies board recipes.
